# Library: proven live-data charts

Every chart here runs on the model **(Sample) Retail - Apparel**, reads only what its search returns (data mode B, an empty state names the search), is interactive, and passed the loop, the edge checks, `probe.mjs` and a screenshot in a real cluster. Start from the nearest one: copy `library/<slug>/`, change the search and the copy, run the loop.

Shared pieces: `library/_shared/core.js` and `core.css` (theme, data access, tooltip, bounded CDN loader, boot), `library/_shared/us-states.js` (inline US map). The Liveboard that arranges them, and the tools that build it, are in the sibling skill `ts-object-liveboard-chart-builder`.

Charts marked *(preview only)* were built on real search output and passed the loop, the edge checks and `probe.mjs`, but have not been on a Liveboard or screenshotted in a cluster yet (a `.preview-only` file in the chart folder; delete it once the chart has been seen on a tile).

## What each one does when you touch it

- **about-guide**: Click a tab to see how to read it. Hover a family for its item types and shares; click a family to dim the rest.
- **about-hero**: Click a person or a term to expand it.
- **banner-pulse**: Hover a number for how it is calculated. Click a prompt to copy it, then paste it into Spotter.
- **kpi-bullet**: Hover the sparkline for each month's sales and whether it sits below the average, above it, or at the best month; arrow keys step through the months. The headline counts up on first draw.
- **kpi-dotstrip**: Hover a dot for its month and sales. Click a dot to select it and dim the rest (Escape clears). Click a legend key to select that whole group (below, above or the latest month). The chart is keyboard navigable.
- **kpi-flip**: Hover a bar for the month, its sales and whether it is above or below the average month. Click a bar to select it and dim the rest (Escape clears).
- **kpi-odometer**: Hover the sparkline for each year's figure over the same months; arrow keys step through the years. The headline counts up on first draw.
- **kpi-quarter-pairs**: Sales and Units sold toggle. Hover, or use the arrow keys, for a pair. Click a quarter (or Enter) to drill into its months, paired the same way; Back, Escape or a click on empty space returns. Click a legend key to hide a year.
- **kpi-ring**: Hover the dial for the share of last year and its status. The arc sweeps in on first draw.
- **kpi-sparkbars**: Hover, or use the arrow keys, for a month's sales and the same month a year earlier. Click a column (or Enter) to select it; Escape clears. Click the legend key to hide the year-earlier ghosts.
- **pulse-family-growth**: The measure toggle switches between latest full year and year to date. Hover a bar for the units effect, the price effect and their total. Click a family (or Enter) to drill into its item types; Back, Escape or a click on empty space returns. Click a legend key to hide an effect. Arrow keys step through families.
- **pulse-pace-lines**: Hover a month for every year's cumulative sales. Click a point on a line to select it (Escape clears). On narrow tiles the legend names the years, and clicking a key hides or shows a year; wider tiles name each line at its right end with its total. Arrow keys step through months and years.
- **pulse-region-bridge**: Hover or arrow-key to a bar for the region's change in sales, or a YTD total. Click bars to select several; Escape clears.
- **pulse-kpi-asp**: Hover the sparkline, or focus the tile and use the arrow keys, for each year's total over the same months; Space pins the tooltip. The headline counts up on the first draw.
- **pulse-kpi-best**: Hover, or use the arrow keys, for each month's sales. Drag across the plot to zoom into a span of months; double-click or Escape resets.
- **pulse-kpi-sales**: Hover the sparkline, or focus the tile and use the arrow keys, for each year's total over the same months; Space pins the tooltip. The headline counts up on the first draw.
- **pulse-kpi-units**: Hover the sparkline, or focus the tile and use the arrow keys, for each year's total over the same months; Space pins the tooltip. The headline counts up on the first draw.
- **pulse-monthly-line**: Hover for the crosshair and a tooltip with the month, sales and the same month last year. Click a legend key (shown on narrow tiles; wider tiles name each line at its right end) to hide or show a line. Drag across the plot to zoom into a span of months; double-click or Escape resets. The chart is keyboard navigable: arrow keys step through months and between the two lines.
- **banner-where**: Hover a number for how it is calculated. Click a prompt to copy it, then paste it into Spotter.
- **state-sales-units**: Hover or arrow-key to a dot for its state, sales, units and sales per unit (other dots dim); click dots to select several, Escape clears; drag to zoom, double-click or Escape resets. The dashed diagonal is the average price: states above it sell at more per unit.
- **where-hex-cartogram**: Click Sales or Share of total to switch the measure (fill, legend, subtitle and tooltip follow). Hover or arrow-key to a hex for the state and its value; the hovered hex is outlined. Click hexes to select several; Escape clears. States without sales are drawn as empty outlines.
- **where-region-item-heatmap**: Click Sales or Share of region to switch the measure. Hover or arrow-key to a cell for the item type, region and value; the cell is outlined. Click cells to select several; Escape clears.
- **where-region-pivot**: Hover or arrow-key to a cell for its region (or state), family and price per unit. Click a cell, or focus it and press Enter, to drill into that region's states; the breadcrumb, Back, Escape or a click on empty space goes back up.
- **where-state-bubbles**: Click Sales or Stores to switch what the fill shows (summed sales, or the number of stores in the state). Hover or arrow-key to a state for its value; click states to select several, Escape clears.
- **where-store-dumbbell**: Hover or arrow-key to a store for last year and this year; the other stores dim. Click stores to select several; Escape clears. Click a year in the legend to hide it.
- **where-store-wall**: Click Same scale or Own scale: Same scale colours each quarter by its sales, Own scale by its share of that store's total, so the shape of each store compares regardless of size. Hover or arrow-key to a cell for the store, quarter and value. Click cells to select several; Escape clears.
- **banner-what**: Hover a number for how it is calculated. Click a prompt to copy it, then paste it into Spotter.
- **what-chord**: Hover or arrow-key to a region or family arc for its sales; the ribbons it touches stay lit and the rest dim. Hover a ribbon for that region-to-family flow. Click a region arc (or focus it and press Enter) to drill into it: the region stays on the left and its flows fan out to the families. The breadcrumb reads Back > region, and Back, Escape or a click on empty space goes back up.
- **what-marimekko**: Hover or arrow-key to a block for its region, with every family's sales and share of that region in the tooltip; the other blocks dim. Click a block (or Enter) to select it, and click a family in the legend to select that family across every region; Escape clears.
- **what-money-sankey**: Hover or arrow-key to a node or flow for its sales and to highlight the flows it touches (the rest dim). Click a region node to drill into that region: it stays as the first column with its family and item type flows. Click a family there to drill one column further into its item types. The breadcrumb shows Back > region > family, and Back, Escape or a click on empty space goes up one level.
- **what-pareto**: Hover or arrow-key to a bar for the product, its rank, sales, units and the running share; the crosshair marks its point on the cumulative line. Play steps the scope through the six largest item types and ends on All (the default view); each scope re-ranks its own products with its own 80% marker. Click a bar (or Enter) to select it; Escape clears.
- **what-premium-diverging**: Hover or focus a bar for its tooltip: the gap in points with Premium or Volume, plus the share of sales and the share of units behind it; the other bars dim. Click a bar, or focus it and press Enter, to drill into that item type's products; the breadcrumb, Back, Escape or a click on empty space goes back up. Arrow keys move between bars. The subtitle names the leader of the level shown and follows the drill. All of this is built into MayaCharts.
- **what-price-volume**: Hover or arrow-key to a bubble for its item type, family, units, price and sales, with crosshair values on both axes. Click a bubble (or Enter) to select it; click a family in the legend to select (isolate) that family; Escape clears. Drag across the plot to zoom; double-click or Escape resets.
- **what-quadrant-guide**: Hover or arrow-key to a quadrant bar for its sales by family and units; the other bars dim. Click a quadrant (or Enter) to drill into its item types, each with its sales and units; the breadcrumb, Back, Escape or a click on empty space goes back up. Click a family in the legend to hide or show it.
- **what-radial-bars**: Hover or arrow-key to a bar for its item type and sales; the other bars dim. Click a bar (or Enter) to select it, and click a family in the legend to select that family's item types; Escape clears.
- **what-treemap-drill**: The root shows each family with its item types nested inside under a name strip. Click anywhere in a family to zoom into it (its item types with their products nested inside), then click an item type to zoom into its products; the breadcrumb reads Back > Family > Item type, and Back, Escape or a click on empty space goes up one level. Hover or arrow-key to a rectangle for its sales (others dim). The product level is the last: a click there does nothing.
- **what-sunburst**: Hover a wedge for its sales and share of the parent (other wedges dim). Click a wedge to zoom into it (animated), with a breadcrumb of the path; Back, Escape or a click on empty space goes up a level. The centre shows the total of the current level.
- **banner-when**: Hover a number for how it is calculated. Click a prompt to copy it, then paste it into Spotter.
- **when-bubble-motion**: Press Play (above the legend) to step through the quarters every 0.9 s; it stops on the last quarter, which is the one shown at first, and the title names the quarter on screen. The axes span every quarter, so the bubbles move against a still frame. Hover a bubble (or arrow-key to it) for the item type, family, units, price per unit and sales, with crosshair guides to both axes; the other bubbles dim. Click a bubble or a family in the legend to select it, Escape clears.
- **when-calendar-heatmap**: Toggle Sales / Units / Price per unit above the grid; the cells re-colour and the ramp key rescales to the selected measure. Hover a cell (or arrow-key to it) for the month, year and value. Click a cell to select it, Escape clears.
- **when-family-stream**: Toggle Sales / Share of quarter: Sales stacks the families in dollars, Share stacks them to 100% of each quarter. Hover a quarter for every family's value; click a family in the legend (narrow tiles) to hide or show it, wide tiles name each family at its right end. Drag across the plot to zoom into a span of quarters; double-click or Escape resets.
- **when-rank-bump**: Hover a dot (or arrow-key to it) to light that item type's thread and dim the others; the tooltip lists that year's whole order with each rank and sales. Click a dot or thread to select it, Escape clears. Where two threads cross, the one that climbs passes over the one that falls.
- **when-ridgeline**: Hover a month for every year's sales in that month, with a vertical guide and a dot on each ridge. Click a year in the legend to hide or show its ridge. Arrow keys step through months and years.
- **when-seasonal-radial**: Hover a segment (or arrow-key to it) for that month's sales in every year; the month is outlined and the rest dims. Click a segment or a year in the legend to select that year, Escape clears. The centre shows total sales.
- **banner-who**: Hover a number for how it is calculated. Click a prompt to copy it, then paste it into Spotter.
- **who-item-waffle**: Switch between Sales and Units with the toggle above the grid; the key keeps each family's share of the shown measure. Hover a square or a key entry for the family, its value and share. Click a family (a square or its key entry) to select it and dim the rest; Escape clears. The chart is keyboard navigable.
- **who-product-beeswarm**: Hover a dot for the product, its item type and its price per unit. Click dots to select several (the rest dim); Escape clears. Click a family in the key to hide or show its lane. Arrow keys step through the dots.
- **who-product-table**: Click a column header (or Enter / Space on it) to sort by that column; click again to flip the order. Hover a row's bar for a tooltip with its sales, units, average price and price against its item type. Click a row to select it; Escape clears. Arrow keys move between rows.
- **who-region-profile**: Switch between Gap from average and Share of region sales with the toggle. Hover a bar for the region, the family and the value. Click a family in the key to hide or show it. Click bars to select; Escape clears. Arrow keys step through regions and families.
- **who-store-league**: Click a column header (or Enter / Space on it) to sort by store, YTD sales, change on last year, movement or form; rows start in league order (YTD sales, highest first). Hover a row's bar for a tooltip with all four values. Click a row to select it; Escape clears. Arrow keys move between rows.
- **who-store-parallel**: Hover a line for the store and its five values. Click lines to select several (the rest fade); Escape clears. Arrow keys step between stores. Store names label the line ends where they fit.
- **who-store-race**: Press Play to step through the quarters (every 900 ms); bars re-rank and the title follows the leader of the quarter shown. Hover a bar for the store, its cumulative sales and that quarter's sales. Click stores to select them; Escape clears. Arrow keys step between bars.
- **banner-next**: Hover a number for how it is calculated. Click a prompt to copy it, then paste it into Spotter.
- **next-action-list**: click a move to open or close its evidence; hover an evidence bar for the numbers
- **next-growth-matrix**: Hover a bubble for the item type, its family, share of sales, sales per unit and sales. The measure toggle above the plot switches the vertical axis between sales per unit and change on the same quarters last year. Click bubbles to select several (the others dim; Escape clears); click a family in the legend to hide or show it. Drag to zoom; double-click or Escape resets. Arrow keys step through the bubbles.
- **next-run-rate**: Hover a month for the actual value or both projected values. Click a legend key to hide or show a scenario, which replaces the old scenario toggle. Wider tiles also name each line at its right end. Drag to zoom into a span of months; double-click or Escape resets. Arrow keys step through months and lines.
- **next-stores-at-risk**: Hover a store for its change on last year (with an above or below the line tone), sales this year and sales last year. Click stores to select them (Escape clears). Arrow keys step through stores. The threshold is the `THRESHOLD` constant at the top of the body (default -5%), not a slider.
- **next-what-if**: Drag or arrow-key the price and units sliders: the projected columns and the year-end totals glide to the new figure. Reset returns to the baseline. Hover a month for baseline, scenario and change.
